TIDE GUIDE

Choose and complete tasks

Use Daily, Weekly, or Monthly to switch task lists. Select a task to complete it and earn coins and experience. Priority tasks are marked and award extra experience.

Elements

Open Elements for Refresh Tasks, Additional Quizzes, My Inner Tide, Aquarium List, and Aquarium Status. Refresh Tasks replaces tasks for the selected period when a refresh is available.

Aquarium List

Use the arrows to browse your tanks and Choose to enter an unlocked aquarium. Fill a tank to 100% to unlock the next one. A completed tank also lets you continue or start a new aquarium.

Profile and settings

Your profile shows your level and experience progress. Settings include graphics, reduced motion, letterboxing, music and sound volumes, account settings, and data options. Credits lists the team.

Task Menu tab

Return to your tasks. Open Elements there for quizzes, your aquarium list, and aquarium status.

Tank Store

Spend coins on fish and aquarium objects. The Misc section also has small and large food pellets.

Editor

Open Editor, choose a layer, then select an item from your inventory. Click the tank to place it, or drag a placed fish or object to move it. Drag a placed item to the editor toolbar to return it to inventory. Each tank can hold up to 10 fish.

Actions and feeding

Choose Actions, open the food picker, and select a pellet. Click in the tank to drop it; fish may swim over to eat it. Use the close button at the top left to leave feeding mode.

Fish, glass, and music

Click a fish to zoom in and see its information and facts. Tap empty water to make fish react; repeated taps can startle them. Click the record player to choose an aquarium track. If you close it without choosing, the current track resumes.

Journals

Open Journals from the aquarium to read, create, and delete journal entries.

Hide

Hide the aquarium controls for a cleaner view. Use the Show button to bring them back.

Tank completion

At 100% completion, choose Continue to keep viewing your tank or Start a New Aquarium to open the aquarium list.

Choose a layer

Use the layer controls to switch between water, fish, and sand layers. The item strip shows recent items on the selected layer; open the chest to browse your full inventory.

Place and organize items

Select a fish or object, then click an open spot in the tank to place it. Drag placed items to reposition them, or drag them to the editor toolbar to return them to inventory. Each tank can hold up to 10 fish.

Finish editing

Use the close button in the editor toolbar to exit. If available, Hide Other Layers isolates the selected layer while you work.

Feed your fish

Open the food picker with the bag button, then choose a small or large pellet. The picker shows the pellet cost and how many you have.

Drop a pellet

After choosing a pellet, click in the tank to drop it. Fish may swim over and eat it. To get more pellets, visit Tank Store and open Misc.

Leave Actions

Use the close button at the top left to leave feeding mode and return to the aquarium.

Choose a quiz

Switch between Single Category and Multi-Category quizzes, then select a quiz card to begin. Cards show the question count, coin reward, and completion progress. Priority quizzes are marked and show their reward bonus.

Navigation

Use the Task Menu or Aquarium tabs to return to those areas. Your coin total is shown in the corner.

Choose an answer

For each statement, choose Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, or Always. The fish marks your selected answer. You need to answer before you can continue.

Move through the quiz

Use the right arrow to save your answer and continue. Use the left arrow to revisit an earlier question; your saved answer will still be selected. The question number shows your place in the quiz.

Finish or quit

After the final answer, review your coin reward before returning to the Quiz Menu. On a regular quiz, use Quit to leave early.
