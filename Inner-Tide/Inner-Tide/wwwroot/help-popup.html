TIDE GUIDE

Tasks

Choose Daily, Weekly, or Monthly to view tasks for that period. Complete a task to earn its coin reward.

Elements

Open Elements for task actions, the Aquarium List, and your current aquarium status. Regenerate Tasks replaces your available tasks.

Aquarium List

Browse your aquariums with the arrows and choose one to enter. A new aquarium unlocks after the previous aquarium reaches 100%.

Profile and options

Open your profile for account options. Settings control graphics, motion, and sound; Credits shows the people behind Inner Tide.

Task Menu tab

Return to your tasks and rewards.

Tank Store

Browse fish and aquarium objects, then use coins to purchase items for your collection.

Inventory

Select a fish from your inventory, then click a spot in the aquarium to place it. Fish already in the aquarium can be dragged to reposition them.

Remove

Grab a fish in the aquarium and drop it on the Remove button to return it to your inventory.

Hide

Hide the aquarium controls for a cleaner view. Use the close button to bring them back.

Quiz Menu

Select a quiz card to begin. The card shows the estimated time, coin reward, and your completion progress.

Navigation

Use Task Menu or Aquarium at the top to return to those areas. Your coin total is shown in the corner.

Answer choices

Choose the response that best matches how often the statement applies to you, from Never to Always. You must answer before moving on.

Move through the quiz

Use the right arrow to continue and the left arrow to revisit an earlier question. Your fish marks the answer you selected.

Quit

Select Quit to leave the quiz and return to the Quiz Menu.
