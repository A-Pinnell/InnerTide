P3 - Version 2.0
- Additional Quizzes are now acessiable, allowing you to curate your My Inner Tide stats to be more specific to you.
- The Regenerate Tasks button now works as initally planned, seperated based on date category and resetting per day/week/month depending on date category
- Tapping in empty spots in the Aquarium will now attract fish or scare away fish based on number of taps
- Inventory button on Aquarium has been removed
- Moving fish in the Aquarium has been removed
- Clicking on a fish will now zoom onto the fish and show information and a fact about it!
- Tank Store icon is now consistent with button theming
- Fish rotation is now smoother
- The editor menu now exists, controlling the placing and moving of fish and objects
    - The editor menu will now show the last eight placed fish/objects
    - The inventory menu has been moved to the editor menu
    - You are now able to place objects in a grid-based space
- The inventory menu will now stack multiples of the same fish/objects
- It is now possible to download your account data from the database in the Data Settings tab contained in the options
- It is now possible to delete your account and data from the databases in the Data Settings tab contained in the options
- It is now possible to wipe your data without wiping your account from the databases in the Data Settings tab contained in the options
- Sound Effects have been added to places where it wasn't prior
- Transitions have been made smoother and improved
- More fish, more objects!
- Various bug fixes
