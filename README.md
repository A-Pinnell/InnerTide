# InnerTide

As technology becomes more accessible, mental wellness apps and chatbots are increasingly common. However, some users hesitate to use them because of their clinical design or lack of motivation to return.

Inner Tide takes a more engaging, game-like approach inspired by digital pets. Users complete daily, weekly, and monthly wellness tasks to earn currency, which they can use to build and personalize a digital aquarium with fish, decorations, and backdrops. The app also encourages journaling and offers quizzes on various mental and physical health topics, creating a more personal and lasting connection with the user.
